# Video Script — Problem → Solution (marketing/story video)

**Length target:** 60–90 seconds. Tone: relatable frustration → relief. Voiceover + on-screen text.

---

### SCENE 1 — The queue (0:00–0:15)
**Visual:** A crowded cyber café / xerox-and-internet shop. A queue of people holding folders
stuffed with photocopies — Aadhaar card, PAN card, marksheets, passport photos. One person wipes
sweat, checks their phone, sighs. A shop worker is scanning documents one by one, slowly.

**On-screen text:** *"Every form. Every time. Same documents."*

**VO (tired, matter-of-fact):**
> "Admission form. Scholarship form. Passport form. Every single time — the same PAN card, the
> same marksheet, the same passport photo… printed, scanned, uploaded, again."

---

### SCENE 2 — The frustration montage (0:15–0:30)
**Visual:** Quick cuts — someone re-typing their date of birth for the fifth form this month;
someone hunting through a phone gallery for a scanned Aadhaar photo; a cyber café operator saying
"scan yeah scan again" while the customer looks defeated; a clock speeding up to show time passing
in the queue.

**On-screen text:** *"Hours lost. Same data. Every form."*

**VO:**
> "Hours in queues. Money for photocopies. And the same information, typed out again and again —
> for forms that are asking for exactly what you already gave somewhere else."

---

### SCENE 3 — The turn (0:30–0:38)
**Visual:** Screen goes calm. A phone lights up with the **DocFill** logo. Cut to a laptop showing
a clean form with a small **"⚡ Autofill with DocFill"** button.

**On-screen text:** *"What if you never had to type it again?"*

**VO (hopeful, upbeat):**
> "What if your documents lived in one place you already trust — your own Google Drive — and any
> form could ask for exactly what it needs, with your permission, in one tap?"

---

### SCENE 4 — The demo (0:38–1:05)
**Visual (screen recording):**
1. Form on a laptop — click **"⚡ Autofill with DocFill."** A QR appears in a clean modal.
2. Cut to a phone: open the DocFill app, scan the QR.
3. Phone shows: *"This form is requesting: Full name, PAN, 12th Marksheet."* with a green
   checklist.
4. Tap **Approve & Send**.
5. Cut back to laptop: fields fill in real-time — name types in, PAN appears, and the **12th
   marksheet fills into the actual file upload box** with a green "✓ Uploaded" tag.

**On-screen text (appears with each step):**
- *"Scan."*
- *"See exactly what's requested."*
- *"Approve."*
- *"Filled — for real. No re-uploading."*

**VO:**
> "Scan the QR. See exactly what the form is asking for. Approve it — and watch your form fill
> itself. Even the documents. No re-uploading. No retyping."

---

### SCENE 5 — The trust line (1:05–1:15)
**Visual:** Simple animated diagram — a phone icon with a small lock, connecting only to the exact
file the user picked, with the rest of a Google Drive folder greyed out/blocked.

**On-screen text:** *"Only the files you choose. Never your whole Drive."*

**VO:**
> "DocFill can only ever see the documents you personally selected — never your whole Drive. And
> your files stay in your Drive. Always."

---

### SCENE 6 — Close (1:15–1:25)
**Visual:** DocFill logo. Tagline. Optional: cyber café worker now relaxed, scrolling on phone,
queue much shorter.

**On-screen text:** **DocFill — Scan once. Fill anywhere.**

**VO:**
> "DocFill. Scan once. Fill anywhere."

---
